# Free Kick Legends 3D

A browser-based 3D free kick game built with three.js.

## Play
Open `index.html` in a browser, or play it on GitHub Pages.

## Features
- Walk mode: dribble anywhere on the pitch and take the free kick from that spot (must be outside the box)
- Custom free kicks: set the distance, angle, wall size and whether the wall jumps
- Legends: Roberto Carlos (left foot, outside-of-the-foot specialist) and Sidny Lopes Cabral (two-footed)
- Outside-of-the-foot (trivela) shots that bend the opposite way to a normal curler
- Choose your kicking foot (weak foot lowers Curve, Accuracy and Power)
- Seven shooting spots from 18 m to 32 m (edge of the box, channels, wide angles, long range, screamer)
- Curve, knuckle, dip, normal and power shots with real ball flight
- Goalkeeper AI with three difficulty levels, plus a jumping wall
- Coins for goals, with bonuses for perfect timing, top corners, streaks and gold target rings
- Upgradeable Power, Curve, Accuracy and Control stats, saved in your browser
- Wind, slow-mo finishes, sound effects and confetti

## Controls
Walk mode: WASD / arrow keys to walk (Shift to sprint) or click the pitch, then press E to take the free kick there. Press E again to walk somewhere new.

Move the mouse to aim. Hold the mouse button or SPACE to charge, release to shoot. Arrow keys / WASD fine-tune aim.

## Game modes (v4)

- **Free kicks** - preset spots, custom free kicks, or walk anywhere to place the ball.
- **Match play** - run with the ball on your feet past three defenders, beat them with skill moves and finish with any shot type. A presser tackles you if you dawdle; beaten defenders get a bonus.
- **Practice arena** - fenced training pitch with cones, dummies and a target. Toggle the goalkeeper and dummies, and score practice points with no pressure.

### Match play / practice controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Dribble |
| Shift | Sprint |
| Mouse | Aim |
| Hold click or Space | Charge and shoot (timed finishing) |
| 1-6 | Normal, curve, knuckle, dip, power, outside foot |
| Q / R / F / C / X / Z / G | Step-over, roulette, elastico, ball roll, drag back, rainbow flick, fake shot |

## v5: Legends, replays and more

- **25 takers**, grouped as Legends (Roberto Carlos, Beckham, Juninho, Pirlo, Ronaldinho, Zico, Platini, Koeman, Mihajlovic, Del Piero), Modern stars (Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Bale, Neymar, Ward-Prowse, Alexander-Arnold, Dybala, Calhanoglu, Sidny Lopes Cabral) and the originals. Each has their own kit, hair, preferred foot, signature technique, overall rating and goal celebration.
- **Goal replays** play automatically after every goal: slow motion, letterbox and five cameras (Auto, Broadcast, Behind the goal, Ball cam, Keeper view). Press V to switch camera, Space to skip, P (or the button) to watch your last goal again.
- **Celebrations**: knee slides, airplane runs, the jump-and-spin, pointing to the sky, and more.
- **Weather and time of day**: sunny, sunset, night under floodlights, rain and snow. Rain and snow make the ball slippery, so keepers make more mistakes.
- **Achievements and records**: 18 achievements with coin rewards, plus your fastest shot, longest goal and more.
- **Shot card** showing speed (km/h) and distance on every strike.
